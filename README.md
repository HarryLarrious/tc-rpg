# tc-rpg: Valenmoor

A third-person fantasy RPG for DiamondFire, written in
[Terracotta](https://github.com/Owlfroggy/terracotta). Plot 35000113 (Mega:
1001 x 1001 build area, 300 x 300 code space). Minecraft 26.3, resource pack
format 97.1. Design notes and decisions: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

## What is in it

- **Third-person camera**: you ride a camera boom behind your character;
  WASD moves relative to the camera. Your character is a custom model
  (six item displays) on an invisible body that vanilla physics moves.
- **Character designer**: race, body, height, hair, beard and colors for
  skin, hair, eyes and clothes, previewed live.
- **Eight races**: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Demon and the beast-folk Felkin
  (cat), Lupin (wolf), Ursan (bear) and Leporin (rabbit), each with its own
  bonuses, ears, horns or tails.
- **Gear**: 17 weapon types (sword, longsword, greatsword, dagger, spear,
  halberd, axe, greataxe, mace, warhammer, scythe, gauntlets, staff, wand,
  bow, crossbow, shield) in 9 materials and 6 rarities, and 8 armor sets
  for head, body, arms and legs. Gear is drawn on your character.
- **Combat**: three-hit combos and heavy attacks per weapon class, dodge
  rolls with invulnerability, lock-on, critical hits, hitstop, camera
  shake, slash trails and damage numbers. Monsters telegraph their attacks.
- **Word magic**: spells are phrases of Latin words. Each word changes the
  spell: elements (ignis, glacies, fulmen...), forms (sagitta, globus,
  radius, anulus, murus, pluvia, nubes, lancea, scutum), verbs (ferio,
  sano, traho, pello, ligo, volo, voco) and modifiers (magnus, celer,
  multus, sequens, rumpens...). Inscribe phrases into your wand's or
  staff's slots at the Spell Loom, or speak them in chat. INT makes magic
  stronger and cheaper.
- **Weapon arts**: Lunge, Whirlwind, Cleave, Shadowstep, Earthshaker,
  Volley, War Cry, Second Wind, in ability slots.
- **The world**: generated by the plot from seed 7341 and sized from the
  plot size: hills, a river with bridges, a lake, forests, and the walled
  city of Valenmoor with streets, a market square, gatehouses, farms and
  about 150 buildings (armory, library, blacksmith, adventurers' guild,
  inn, lord's keep, chapel, tavern, alchemist, store, bakery, mage tower,
  barracks, stables, houses), each furnished for its use. Outside: a
  bandit camp, a spider cave, a druid grove, and ruins over a crypt.
- **Townsfolk**: workers, guards and villagers of every race with homes,
  jobs and needs. A utility-based decision model chooses between work,
  meals, rest, company, wandering, patrols and fleeing; they walk the
  street graph. Talk to them with F.
- **Progression**: levels, stat points, gold, shops, repairs and rarity
  upgrades, words from the library, arts from the guild trainer, guild
  contracts ranked F to S, and the lord's story of the three threats.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D, mouse | Move, look |
| Space / Ctrl | Jump / sprint |
| Shift | Dodge roll |
| Left / right click | Light combo / heavy attack (bows: quick / drawn shot) |
| 2-8 | Spells and arts in your slots |
| 9 | Journal: character, equipment & bag, Spell Loom, words, arts, quests |
| F | Talk |
| Q | Lock on |

## Building

```bash
~/.claude/skills/terracotta/scripts/tc-check.sh tc
```

```bash
tools/release_pack.sh
```

```bash
~/.claude/skills/terracotta/scripts/tc-send.py tc
```

On the plot, `@world build` generates the world (it resumes by itself
after a restart; `@world status` shows progress). When it finishes the
townsfolk move in.

## Admin commands

`@world build|stop|status|reset|replan|phase NAME`, `@npc spawn|clear`,
`@mob TYPE [LVL]`, `@mob clear`, `@give TYPE [TIER RARITY]`,
`@give armor SLOT SET [TIER RARITY]`, `@eq TYPE [TIER RARITY]`,
`@eqa SET [TIER RARITY]`, `@loot [LVL]`, `@xp N`, `@gold N`, `@learn`,
`@cam DIST`, `@tp X Y Z`, `@design`, `@respawn`.
